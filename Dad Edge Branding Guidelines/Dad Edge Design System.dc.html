The rules and components for applying the Dad Edge brand to web applications and sites. Direction: Tactical Balance — dark-first, grit reserved for hero moments, a calm working UI everywhere else.

01 Logo
Master lockup. App headers, marketing, footers. White on dark, black on light — never colored.
Centurion mark. Favicons, avatars, badges, loading states, tight spaces < 120px wide.
Tagline lockup. Cover moments only: login, landing hero, onboarding. Never below 220px wide.
Do — keep clear space equal to the wordmark height on all sides; use black or white only; place on solid black, near-black surfaces, or photography with a dark overlay.
Don't — stretch, recolor, add glows or shadows, place on busy backgrounds, or pair with other marks closer than the clear space.
02 Color

03 Typography
Oswald
Display & headings — free stand-in for Trade Gothic Bold. Weights 500–700. Uppercase with 1–3px letter-spacing for headers and labels; sentence case allowed at 32px+.
Montserrat
Body & UI — free stand-in for Proxima Nova. Weights 400–700. Everything that isn't a heading: paragraphs, controls, data, captions.

04 Texture & Grit
The brand's grunge texture is an accent, not wallpaper. Rule of thumb: one textured surface per screen — the hero, a cover, or a celebration moment — at 12–18% opacity on black. Working surfaces (cards, forms, tables, lists) stay clean.
Hero surface
Yes — white texture at 12–18% on pure black, behind display type.
Celebration
Yes — dark texture over an accent fill for level-ups, badges earned, streak milestones.
Form fields, body copy, and data tables with texture running underneath the reading surface…
No — never behind body text, forms, or data. Working surfaces stay clean.

07 Layout Patterns
App shell. Black sidebar (240px) + textured black hero + clean two-column working area (2:1).
Marketing page. Full-bleed textured hero with display type, 3-up card rows, cyan CTA. Generous 96px+ section spacing.
Auth / focus. Single centered card on textured black, tagline lockup above, one action.
Spacing
Base unit 4px. Card padding 24–28px. Grid gaps 16–20px in-app, 24+ marketing. Section rhythm 72–96px.
Corners & depth
Square corners everywhere; circles reserved for avatars, badges, checkmarks, toggles. No drop shadows — hierarchy comes from surface steps and borders.
Motion
Fast and firm: 120–180ms ease-out on hover/press. Celebrations (badge, level-up) may go bigger: 400ms with texture reveal.
08 Voice & Copy
Sounds like us
“Win today, Marcus.”
“3 battles left. Finish strong.”
“Streak at risk — don't break the chain.”
“Hold the line. 3 days to Relentless.”
Doesn't
“Oops! Something went wrong 😅”
“You're crushing it, superstar!!”
“Gentle reminder to check in…”
Corporate jargon, hedging, emoji.
Direct, brotherly, earned — a coach who respects you. Short sentences. Second person. Military/training metaphors used with restraint (battles, streaks, squads), never cosplay. Address the man, not the metric.
